Food for All Initiative

We are a Nairobi-based non-governmental organization dedicated to combating food insecurity and eradicating hunger. Through sustainable agricultural support and direct food distribution, we ensure that vulnerable communities across Nairobi have consistent access to nutritious meals.

Our Programmes

  • Urban Farming Workshops
  • School Feeding Outreach
  • Community Food Rescue
